This policy explains how the mobile game Cannon Castle (“the App”) handles user information. The App is a free, ad-supported game made by an individual developer.

1. Information the developer collects — none

The App does not collect personal information or send it to the developer. In particular, the App has none of the following:

  • Sign-up, login, or account system
  • Servers or databases operated by the developer
  • Fields for identifying information such as name, email, phone number, or address
  • Access to location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone

Information stored only on your device

So that you can continue your game, the App stores the following only on your device. It is never transmitted off the device.

  • Game progress (current stage, abilities you hold, etc.)
  • Permanent perks you have earned
  • Settings (music, sound effects, vibration, language)

Uninstalling the App, or clearing its data in your device settings, deletes this information as well.

2. Advertising — Google AdMob

The App uses Google AdMob, Google’s mobile advertising service, to show the following ads:

  • A banner ad at the bottom of the screen
  • Rewarded ads: these play only when you choose “Watch Ad to Revive” or “Watch Ad for 2x”, and an in-game reward is given when you watch to the end.

To serve these ads, Google may collect and use the following information directly from your device, without it passing through the developer.

3. Ad privacy consent

If you are in a region where consent is required, such as the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the App shows an ad privacy consent form from Google’s consent management tool (User Messaging Platform) the first time you open it. Depending on your choice you will see personalized or non-personalized ads. You can play the game even if you do not consent.

You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad Privacy Settings in the App. (This option appears only in regions where consent is required.)

The App does not request sensitive permissions such as camera, location, contacts, storage, or microphone.

5. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (under 14 in the Republic of Korea). The developer does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the App, please contact us at the address below and we will look into it.

6. Your choices

  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings → Privacy → Ads. Deleting it stops personalized ads; you will see less relevant, general ads instead.
  • On iOS, you can turn off tracking for the App at any time under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. When it is off, the IDFA is not used for ads.
  • If you are in a region where consent is required, you can change your ad consent under Settings → Ad Privacy Settings in the App.
  • Rewarded ads play only when you choose them.
  • Uninstalling the App immediately stops all processing related to the App and deletes the game data stored on your device.

7. Data retention and deletion

The developer does not retain any user data. Game data stored on your device is deleted when you uninstall the App. Retention and deletion of information collected by Google AdMob are governed by Google’s privacy policy.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised policy and its effective date will be posted on this page. Significant changes will also be noted in the app update description.
